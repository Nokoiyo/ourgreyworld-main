## Our Mission

Our Gray World is an open source table top wargaming system, designed to be community expandable and modifiable. 

All of our 3D STL files are free for personal use and remixing as well.

Our aim is to create the most accessible wargaming experience possible, both in terms of start up and cost. 



## Our Plan

{{% steps %}}

### Step 1

Build a library of STL files, categorized by faction, units, and slots. 

### Step 2

Expand into physical printing, whether through our own means, or through partnerships.
